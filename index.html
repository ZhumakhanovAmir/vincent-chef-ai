💡 Student Chef Tips

🥚 Fluffy Omelet Hack

Add one teaspoon of cold water or milk before you FRY eggs — steam makes them much fluffier!

🍝 Pasta Water Gold

Never pour out all pasta water! Save a cup — the starch makes any sauce creamer and tastier.

🧄 Easy Garlic Peel

Press a garlic clove flat with the side of your knife — the skin comes off in 1 second.
